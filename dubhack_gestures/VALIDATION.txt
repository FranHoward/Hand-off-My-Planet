Manual Acceptance Checks
1. Start with defaults, face forward, and hold the left palm open in a natural neutral position for about one second to calibrate (do not calibrate with a fist).
2. Pushing the open left hand should produce FORWARD; pulling should produce BACKWARD. A fist at any position must immediately produce STOP.
3. Keep the left fist closed and move it forward/backward. move_axis must remain 0; movement can resume after a stable release.
4. An open left palm at neutral must not produce Shield. A stationary open right palm must produce SHIELD.
5. Touch the right thumb and index fingertips together; a brief stable hold should produce LASER. Releasing should end it.
6. Move the pinched hand to aim without triggering Wave/Fire. Extending only the index finger must not produce Laser.
7. Right fist-then-push should still produce Fire, and sideways swipes should still produce Wave. Test left-hand stop alongside right-hand abilities.
8. Head left/center/right and NONE on face loss should retain their previous behavior.
